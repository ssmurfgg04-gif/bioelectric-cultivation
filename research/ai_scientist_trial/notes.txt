# Title: Multi-Pattern Maintenance: Resource Allocation Strategies for Competing Bioelectric Memories
# Experiment description: Modify the experiment to maintain multiple distinct patterns in different body regions simultaneously. Create 3 distinct novel patterns in different zones: (1) a stable pattern with low jump susceptibility, (2) a volatile pattern with high jump susceptibility, and (3) a high-turnover pattern with elevated cell death probability. Implement a resource allocation system in the adaptive_policy arm that must distribute a limited total maintenance budget among these competing patterns. Test allocation strategies: (1) fixed priority (e.g., always prioritize pattern A over B over C), (2) dynamic allocation based on current degradation severity (more resources to the most degraded pattern), (3) balanced allocation (equal resources to all patterns), and (4) critical threshold-based (allocate all resources to any pattern below its critical fidelity threshold). Measure individual pattern recoverability at ages 60 and 100, weighted overall pattern integrity (weighted by pattern importance), median lifespan, and resource allocation efficiency. Test the hypothesis that dynamic allocation strategies will outperform fixed allocation strategies in maintaining overall pattern integrity under resource constraints.
