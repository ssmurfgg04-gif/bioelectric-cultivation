## 1. The layered stack

Every framework we examined — Levin's bioelectricity, the field-resonance
picture, quantum biology, vacuum-energy speculation, morphogenetic-field
ideas, consciousness-collapse interpretations — is a claim about one interface
in a single stack:

```
L5  PHYSICAL          matter, energy, the body           [what physics settles]
L4  BIOELECTRIC       Vmem patterns, gap junctions        [Levin's layer]
L3  INFORMATION       discrete symbols, codes, archives   [Shannon's layer]
L2  QUANTUM           superposition, measurement, noise   [where things are undecided]
L1  CONSCIOUSNESS     integrated information, observation [the unresolved ground]
```

The cultivation narrative ("refine the body, then the energy, then the soul")
is a claim that access can be gained to progressively deeper layers, from the
top down. Science has settled the bottom of this map and the top of it; the
middle interfaces are where the honest unknowns live. The six frameworks map
onto the interfaces: Field-Resonance claims L4→L5 coupling; Quantum-Biology
claims L2→L4 coupling; Morphogenetic-Field claims L3→L5 structure;
Consciousness-Collapse claims L1→L2 causation; Vacuum-Energy claims L2→L5
energy extraction; Layered-Reality is the stack itself, inverted.

**What this repository did**: it made L4→L3→L5 *computable* — the first
integrated model where bioelectric patterns are discrete symbols, aging is
channel + code degradation, and mortality emerges from information loss — and
it ran the arithmetic on the L4→L5 and L1→L2 claims (exp9) that nobody had
bothered to actually compute.

## 2. Layer by layer: what is known

### L4 — Bioelectric (the layer everything hangs on)

**Established** [EST]: membrane voltage is a control layer above genetics.
Vmem patterns instruct morphogenesis (two-headed planaria by 24h perturbation,
with memory across amputations — Durant et al. 2017; reproduced in exp1).
Cancer-like states normalize when gap-junction connectivity is restored
(Chernet & Levin 2015; reproduced in exp2, including the locked-driver
boundary). Frog limb regeneration via a 24h drug cocktail (Morphoceuticals
2022). Planarian regeneration rejuvenates old animals (Dai et al. 2025).
Senescing human keratinocytes show robust depolarization (2025). The
foundational theory: Pio-Lopez & Levin 2024 — *aging as loss of morphostatic
information*. Fields: gap junctions, ion channels, voltage-gated gene
programs. Discreteness: cells occupy Vmem bands whose identity, not proximity,
gates behavior.

**Modeled here** [MODEL]: the 9/9 Level-1 reproductions; Gompertz mortality
emerging from three degradation mechanisms (β ≈ 0.090, the human range);
cross-species lifespan ordering ρ = 1.00; I(V;M) decaying 1.55 → 0.83 bits
with age; and (new this milestone) the fidelity layer — discrete-state
mortality, regional jump corruption, the aging clock (ρ ≈ 0.45), verified
maintenance ×1.09, regeneration ×1.25, the two-bottleneck law.

**The gap nobody had filled**: no computational framework modeled *aging*
(as opposed to development) as bioelectric code degradation. This repository
is that framework, and its falsification ledger is the discipline mechanism.

### L3 — Information (where the codec enters)

**The unified insight** [MODEL]: biology already solved the noisy-channel
problem twice — DNA is a digital, error-corrected, redundant archive (the
outer code); the bioelectric layer is the analog working memory with
gap-junction consensus (the inner code) *but it has no archive of its own*.
The genome stores the youthful pattern; tissue carries a drifting copy.
Aging, in this picture, is the working memory's slow divergence from the
archive — and the exp8 results say the divergence is *discrete* (cells cross
Vmem band boundaries), *regional* (coupled domains flip together), and
*lethal through function* (organs fail on wrong-state fraction, not on
distance).

**Why this explains what confused everyone before**: our own exp6 negative —
"sophisticated error correction ≈ simple maintenance" — was real and is now
resolved: under proximity-based mortality, correctness has no value. Under
fidelity-based mortality (the biologically correct form), verification pays
(codec/local ×1.09, T3.1b PASS), and refuses-to-write becomes a *survival
feature* in noisy channels (T3.1c PASS). The lessons transfer directly from
the helix-codec stress test: no silent writes (F3), intervene before capacity
is exhausted (F5), consensus before correction.

**Why regeneration rejuvenates** [MODEL + EST]: a regeneration event is a
full re-derivation of the pattern from the genomic archive — the one
error-free store in the body. exp8's regen arm (×1.25, the largest single
intervention effect) is the computational version of Dai et al. 2025. And the
model explains their second finding for free: the *channel* engines (noise
growth, junction decay) are not reset by re-derivation — so cycles must be
repeated to maintain youth. Regeneration is a restore-from-backup, not a
reformat of the disk.

**The two-bottleneck law** [MODEL]: pattern repair plateaus when channel
noise crosses the symbol spacing (the Shannon capacity cliff); channel repair
(connexin restoration) re-opens junction-scaled pattern propagation — which
*un-quarantines corrupted domains* (gap-junction decay was accidentally
protective). The value of each therapy INVERTS with the binding constraint:
connexin boost helps ×1.06 when noise binds (cliff probe), hurts ×0.94-vs-
codec when corruption binds. Even full regeneration is channel-limited (×0.77
of its low-noise value). Interventions must be diagnosed, not blindly applied.

### L2 — Quantum (established fringe, speculative core)

**Established** [EST]: biology uses quantum mechanics beyond chemistry —
photosynthetic energy transfer maintains coherence, avian magnetoreception
runs on radical-pair chemistry, enzyme catalysis uses tunneling. Quantum
biology is real, narrow, and thermal-decoherence-bounded.

**The honest state of the speculative part**: no evidence the nervous system
exploits macro-scale quantum coherence; ORCH-OR remains contested; the
measurement problem is unresolved and honest people disagree about whether
consciousness is involved at all. What exp9 adds is not opinion but
arithmetic — see L1.

### L1 — Consciousness (the unresolved ground)

**Established** [EST]: integrated information Φ is computable for small
systems and our model confirms the structural predictions (Φ peaks at
intermediate coupling, ≈0 for modular architectures; 0.12 bits on real
C. elegans gap-junction motifs). Autonomic influence is trainable (Tummo,
Wim Hof, HRV biofeedback): the nervous system can steer visceral function
through practice. Anesthesia's mechanism remains incomplete — we know what it
blocks, not finally what consciousness is.

**The anomaly record, taken straight** [EST as data, interpretation open]:
the Ganzfeld meta-analysis (Stage-2 registered report, 40+ years of studies)
finds ES 0.088, passing four publication-bias tests, ~3× larger in trained
participants, with telepathy-simulating tasks ~2× guessing tasks. PEAR ran
28 years and millions of trials for a ~10⁻⁴ per-trial deviation. These are
tiny, contested, and — this is the point — *nobody has done the mechanism
search* the way it was eventually done for acupuncture (points right, framing
wrong, mechanism = vagus nerve; once found, the intervention was optimizable).

## 3. The exp9 audits: the arithmetic nobody ran

### Audit 1 — the biofield amplification ladder

A cell needs a ~5 mV induced shift (half a Vmem band) to flip discrete state;
that is 333 V/m externally (Schwan). The heart's current dipole delivers
3.2×10³ V/m at 1 cm — **contact-range bioelectric influence between tissues
is real physics** [EST-by-arithmetic]. At 10 cm it is 100× short; at 1 m,
10⁵× short. Closing the 1 m gap needs ~10⁵ phase-locked cardiac-scale dipoles
— 10⁵× the body's entire ionic current budget. Schumann coupling is ~10¹³
short and resonance cannot rescue it (lossy tissue Q ≈ 1-10).

Verdicts [AUDIT]: passive organism-range biofield influence — **DEAD-AS-
STATED** (T3-F2, F3). Contact-range effects — **plausible, unverified at the
bioelectric-outcome level, and testable with existing MEG/MCG-grade
magnetometry plus a cell culture** — an experiment nobody has run.

### Audit 2 — selection vs energy (what micro-PK could actually do)

The energy question and the selection question are different, and conflating
them has wasted decades of argument:

- **Selection**: an amplified quantum decision (an RNG bit) is *already
  macroscopic* — the energy comes from the power supply; the quantum event
  only selects. At PEAR's ε this is a macroscopic statistical bias. That IS
  what PEAR measured. The energy objection never applied to switch-flipping
  [AUDIT].
- **Energy**: moving grams or shattering matter requires redirecting thermal
  events. At ε = 10⁻⁴ with 10⁶ consciously-addressable events/s, lifting 1 g
  by 10 cm takes ~7×10⁸ years. Even biasing *every* thermal event in 10 g of
  tissue with perfect rectification yields ~40 mW. Macro telekinesis is
  **DEAD-AS-STATED** (F4) — not because consciousness is classical, but
  because arithmetic is arithmetic.
- **The convergence nobody noticed**: 40 mW is ~9 orders of magnitude ABOVE
  the 1.25×10⁻¹⁶ J needed to flip a single cell's bioelectric state. If any
  real consciousness-matter interface exists, the first place it could
  physically express is the bioelectric layer — Levin's layer — the layer
  this repository models (F6, OPEN). The cultivation hierarchy's lowest rungs
  and its highest speculation meet at the same tissue interface.

**The acupuncture precedent, applied**: small real effect → wrong framing →
mechanism search → optimized intervention. The audits say the mechanism
search should be pointed at bioelectric-state transitions in cells (pJ-scale,
readable with existing instruments), not at gram-scale matter movement.

## 5. The seven connected answers

**A1 — Aging is information loss, and the math now comes from physics, not
curve-fitting.** Pio-Lopez & Levin 2024 proposed it; this repository derives
Gompertz mortality from three degradation mechanisms, matches the human β,
ranks five species correctly, and measures the information decay (1.55 → 0.83
bits). The model's clock — *discrete pattern fidelity* — predicts remaining
lifespan at ρ ≈ 0.45.

**A2 — Regeneration rejuvenates because it re-derives the pattern from the
one error-free archive in the body.** The regen arm is the largest effect in
the entire framework (×1.25), and the model explains why planarians need
*repeated* cycles: the channel engines keep running.

**A3 — The genome is the outer code; the bioelectric layer is the inner code
that lost its archive.** Aging is what that absence costs. "Cultivation," in
the model, is supplying the missing verification layer — and it only pays
when mortality depends on correctness (the exp6 → exp8 arc is the proof).

**A4 — There is a readable bioelectric aging clock.** Fidelity-at-40 predicts
remaining life. The wet-lab translation is direct: voltage dyes + longitudinal
planarian cohorts. This is the cheapest decisive experiment on the map.

**A5 — Interventions have two bottlenecks (pattern AND channel), and their
value inverts with the binding constraint.** Connexin restoration is harmful
in a corruption-limited organism (quarantine loss) and helpful in a
noise-limited one. Any real anti-aging bioelectric program must diagnose
first. This is a *novel prediction* of the framework — no existing literature
states it.

**A6 — The anomaly data, read honestly, points at Levin's layer.** The
selection/energy separation (Audit 2) shows the PEAR-scale effect, if real,
is already macroscopic in the switch-flipping sense and energetically
compatible ONLY with small-state transitions — i.e., with bioelectric
writing. The 3× dose-response in trained meditators fits a trainable channel.
The acupuncture precedent is the method: find the mechanism, then optimize.

**A7 — What is dead stays dead.** Passive range biofields (10⁵ short),
Schumann resonance (10¹³ short), macro telekinesis (10⁸-10¹⁰ short or
10⁸-years slow). Reporting these as dead is a *service* to the research
program: it concentrates the remaining effort where physics still permits a
signal.

## 7. D3 resolved — the write semantics of death (exp16)

The tower's last open joint (T4/D3: "anchor-pinning fights the codec's writes
on senesced cells") is closed, and the closure produced three findings that
stand on their own (full ledger: FALSIFICATION.md Level 5):

**The matched-channel law.** The v1 latch failed composition (x0.80) because
its memory pin (k_anchor = 50/yr) ran ~100x the bandwidth of the gap-junction
channel it was supposed to back up (~g0*deg ~ 0.4/yr). A backup channel that
dominates the primary channel is not a redundancy — it is a replacement that
fights it: the pin decoupled theta from the live V state and chronically
inflated the |V-theta| senescence hazard (sen@60: 0.048 vs 0.014 no-latch).
The v2 latch forms memories FAST (alpha_latch ~ 20/yr — jumps and codec
writes latch within weeks) and pulls WEAKLY (k_anchor = 0.25/yr, calibrated
by the exp16 C2 sweep: composition x1.000 at 0.25 degrading monotonically to
x0.881 at 50). Composition with codec maintenance: **x1.000**. Under the
biologically-corrected death semantics (broadcast): **x0.991**. The somatic
memory's job is to REMEMBER, not to ENFORCE — enforcement belongs to the
live channels (junctions, codec); a memory that enforces becomes the
pathology (the v1 lesson, and a candidate principle for real
reprogramming-safety: any persistent cell-state memory must be strong enough
to hold a pattern and weak enough to be outvoted by verified correction).

**The star question, answered in this model's currency.** When a cell dies,
is the pattern lost, or written somewhere? Four semantics tested:
erasure (I_recoverable@60 = 0.535), transcription (0.534 — IDENTICAL),
stasis (0.560), broadcast (0.560). The naive star hypothesis — death is a
transcription event, the self moves cell-to-cell — fails, and the failure is
the informative kind: single-cell memory copies are outvoted by the
collective consensus within weeks (the copied anchor's deviation exceeds the
deadzone; the consensus re-derives it; alpha_latch = 20/yr erases the copy).
The pattern is not a collection of cell memories; it is an attractor of the
collective dynamics. Which means: (a) no single cell's death can erase it
(redundancy — death is a transition), and (b) no single cell's transcription
can move it (the self is not cell-local). The pattern survives death through
THREE redundant carriers: the collective bioelectric state, the frozen
anchors of senesced cells (stasis, +2.5% recoverable information vs erasure),
and the genomic archive. For NOVEL patterns (not in the archive) the carriers
are only the first two — which is why novel morphologies need active
maintenance (exp11/exp12). **D3b (exp17) closed this gap**: the codec's
write source is now the memory itself (`target_source="anchored"`) with a
protected tier for deliberate writes — the written pattern holds at 0.91
recoverable at 60 / 0.90 at 100 with +20% median lifespan. The full arc is
closed: a novel body plan can be WRITTEN (exp11), survives cell death
(exp16: distributed redundancy), and is MAINTAINED for a century (exp17:
the anchored target). The remaining honest caveats: the tracker-tier trap
(memory-referenced maintenance of a drifting memory maintains the drift —
protection must be deliberate-write-gated), and wrong memories are
maintained with full conviction (correctness is load-bearing).

**The bystander effect is real in the model — and demographically silent.**
The broadcast semantics (the literature's mechanism: injury depolarization
waves, gap-junction bystander effects, SASP spreading — see
`research/d3_sweep/README.md`) shows a dose-dependent senescence excess at
strong amplitude and a lethal interaction with junction decay (perturbations
linger when healing coupling is gone — the model's version of
connexin-loss-impaired wound healing). But at biological amplitude the four
semantics are demographically indistinguishable (medians within 0.7 yr): the
death-write matters for WHERE the pattern lives, not for how long the
organism lives. Demographics was never going to decide the star question;
the ledger does.

   `infer_vmem()`.
2. **Infer** — ion-transporter gene weights -> channel-class permeabilities
   -> Goldman-Hodgkin-Katz resting potential per cell type (method
   established on neurons: Tripathy 2017, Bernaerts 2025, Huang 2025).

   hyperpolarized (curated-hypothesis pass: neoblasts -53 mV, phagocytes

   ready to be checked against atlas numbers the moment they are loaded.
4. **Connect** — the predicted Vmem map is the input the fidelity model
   needs: band-assignment per cell type -> the fidelity clock's predicted
   aging trajectory per tissue -> intervention targets ranked by
   information-theoretic value, not viability alone.

## 9. The century-hold policy and the wet-lab gate (exp19 + FC1-FC5)

Two more pieces landed with the real-data layer:

**The century-hold search (exp19).** With the anchored stack able to hold
novel targets (exp17), the CEM objective changed from "reach the target"
to "hold the target over a century" (fitness = mean alive x I_recoverable,
ages 30-100). The search (a) beats no-maintenance by +23% held-out, (b)

finds the hold-optimum is a different animal than the reach-optimum (write
in adolescence at ~14.5, start maintenance a decade later, dense but
low-amplitude), and (d) honestly fails to beat the hand-built exp17 policy
(+1.2%): the century-hold landscape is a broad plateau — all eight
single-dimension ablations move hold by <= 0.01. The policy was already
sitting on the plateau's heart; the search proved it rather than improved
it (echoes exp14 D5).

**The fidelity-clock wet-lab program (docs/FIDELITY_CLOCK.md).** D3 was the
blocker for wet-lab design; it is resolved, and the experiment series is
now pre-registered: FC1 (does spatial Vmem fidelity decline with age — the
existence test), FC2 (does baseline fidelity predict regeneration and
survival — the prognosis test, computational rho 0.39-0.45), FC3 (is the
mechanism junction-decay quarantine — innexin RNAi phenocopy + bystander
dye-coupling), FC4 (does a written ectopic Vmem pattern persist >= 7 days
and require junction integrity — the protected tier in tissue), FC5 (does
